27 October 2026. Engineering readiness remains **50/100**. This checkpoint addresses the missing bilateral floor-glute-bridge camera reference by checking additional original publishers. It adds an offline source-intake guard; it does not train a network, alter production or measure human rep precision.

The [DVIDS glute-bridge challenge asset](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/757660/one-minute-glute-bridge-challenge) has a generic public-domain banner and an **asset-specific copyright restriction**. Its notice says “Asset contains copyrighted material” and that portions are “not licensed for distribution.” The specific restriction takes precedence in the intake guard. No raw video is downloaded, no audio-removal workaround is inferred and no author is contacted. The [publisher's general terms](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright) also distinguish government visual information from third-party material; a general banner cannot establish the rights to every portion of an individual asset.

The project [distinguishes application licensing from individual exercise/data grants](https://github.com/wger-project/wger#license).

Seven regression cases cover the specific-restriction/banner conflict, script-only text, missing/restrictive licences, variant substitution, attribution/media mismatch, invalid duration and partial/duplicated catalogues.
