The [original descriptor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5773117/) establishes named relative/absolute angular entities and a YXZ rotation convention, but does not supply the original Nexus model/export configuration or per-joint residual/tracking-validity flags in these TXT archives. The [Vicon standard-model guide](https://help.vicon.com/download/attachments/11250652/Plug-in%20Gait%20Reference%20Guide.pdf) distinguishes clinical output components, rotating coordinate axes and shoulder singularities. Those definitions do not prove that the TXT columns are the same clinical-output representation. Rotation order, serialized component order and a clinical scalar angle are different facts; a successful117-column parse does not establish the latter two. The publisher's Kinect visualization code cannot resolve Vicon semantics.

Conversely, finite numbers alone do not prove observed acquisition. A native-angular backend would need explicit validity/representation semantics before its values could certify a counted repetition.
