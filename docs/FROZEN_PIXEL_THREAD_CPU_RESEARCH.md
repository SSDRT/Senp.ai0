Before and after each estimator call, record a complete attempted census of `/proc/self/task/*/stat`, including original raw lines, TIDs, per-entry read errors, enumeration errors and scan start/end times. Match a delta only for the same TID and recorded starttime, with monotonic original counters. Recycled IDs, born/exited threads and unreadable entries stay explicit and their missing CPU is unknown. Missing reads cannot become zero CPU; zero observed ticks does not prove idleness at coarse clock resolution. Thread names are observed labels, not proof of native operator identity. Aggregate stable-identity ticks by original name and identity and retain every interval, including zeros and races.

Scans are non-atomic and perturb the workload. Record both scan durations, preserve their original bounds around the estimator call, and report CPU deltas as **scan-to-scan brackets including observation overhead**, not isolated native-inference CPU. Process CPU still includes other application threads. Report active/quiet variation, uncertainty and actual counter resolution; do not claim that summed thread counters equal the separately timed process/native interval or that all CPU was observed.
